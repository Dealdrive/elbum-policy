Privacy Policy for EL Num

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 | Developer: EL Games Studio

At EL Games Studio, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how EL Num ("the Application") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile game on Android.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

EL Num is an offline-first puzzle game. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or contacts.

2. Local Device Storage

The Application uses local storage on your device (via AsyncStorage) strictly to enhance your gaming experience. This data never leaves your device:

  • Game Progress: Saved board configurations and active move history so you can resume games.
  • High Scores & Statistics: Best scores, total moves, highest merged tiles, and daily streaks.
  • User Preferences: Sound effects toggles, haptic vibration preferences, and chosen board sizes.

You can permanently delete this data at any time through the in-game Settings screen by tapping "Reset Game Statistics", or by clearing the app's cache and data via your Android device settings.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

EL Num is supported through non-intrusive mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve advertisements, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect certain device-level information:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Coarse (non-precise) IP-based location and language settings
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, and rewarded ad completions)

For more details on how Google handles data collected through mobile ads, please review:
Google Advertising & Privacy Policy | Google Privacy Policy

4. Managing Your Ad Preferences

You can control personalized advertising or reset your advertising identifier on your Android smartphone at any time:

  • Open your device's Settings app.
  • Navigate to Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  • Tap "Reset advertising ID" or toggle "Delete advertising ID".

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

EL Num is designed for general casual audiences. The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly take appropriate measures.

6. Permissions Requested

EL Num requests minimal Android permissions solely required for game functionality and ad delivery:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load and render Google AdMob advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify network availability before attempting to load ads.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used to provide optional tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (can be disabled in Settings).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
